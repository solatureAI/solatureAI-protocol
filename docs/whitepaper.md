# Solature Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

Markets on Solana move faster than most people can read them. Solature exists to make that motion legible: a calm, always-on orbit around the tokens drawing the most attention, with a first seat held for our own token and no claim made before it is recorded.

Mission: show real numbers, as received, and never state a fact the project has not written down.

## Principles

1. One record per external fact, tagged stated, absent or unconfirmed.
2. Unstated records are inert: no link, no copy action, no fetch.
3. Market data is independent of our own contract status.
4. Failure is visible and soft: dashes where data is missing, last good values retained.

## Roadmap

**Phase 1 — Orbit.** Live market board and ticker, branded placeholder seat, address check.

**Phase 2 — Pin.** Contract address stated; the first seat goes live with DexScreener and pump.fun data fetched in parallel.

**Phase 3 — Open.** Public repository published and linked; source-level documentation.

**Phase 4 — Extend.** Additional read-only views built on the same register pattern.
